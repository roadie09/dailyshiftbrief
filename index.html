Before the shift.

A 10 to 15 minute briefing for manufacturing plants. It covers more than safety, with more detail, written to draw the crew in. Plain language. Not rushed. A supervisor can run it as long as they want.

It runs in the browser, or you print it. A Spanish version comes with it. It started in wood-products mills. It now covers manufacturing. Warehousing is next, not this subscription.

OSHA has about 106,000 severe-injury reports on file since 2015, about 34,500 of them in manufacturing (federal-OSHA states only). That's enough for a different brief every weekday.

It helps crews stay alert to what actually hurts people. It's built to hold a crew's attention better than a talk they've heard a dozen times.

Who it's for

  • Shift managers and supervisors

    You run it before the crew starts. Plan on 10 to 15 minutes, or as long as you want.

  • Safety officers

    You get a real-incident topic and a signed sheet every day.

  • HR

    When the account is open, we will store the sign-off: name, crew, and date. The Shift End Test is free and optional. If you use it, we also store the score. Those results are not on this website yet. No SSNs. No medical info. Export and delete are coming soon.

  • Plant managers, general managers, and operations managers

    No IT project. Daily Shift Brief does not connect to plant systems. A browser or a printed PDF is the whole setup.

When the account is open, we will store the sign-off: name, crew, and date. Shift End Test scores are stored only if you use that free add-on. No SSNs, no medical info, no access to your plant systems. The plant will own those records. Export and delete are coming soon. Details are on the security sheet.

What the crew hears

  1. What went wrong, from one real incident in your industry.
  2. The control from that incident. Your qualified person decides what applies on your shift.
  3. Two questions the crew answers out loud.

Then the supervisor signs off. Today's brief matches the plant's industry. See a sample brief.

Optional add-on, free

Shift End Test: free, optional. A short test at the end of the week on everything the crew heard. The results show you who's tuned in and which topics didn't land, so you know what to revisit and how to put it differently next time. It runs on that crew's working days, so nobody has to come in on a day off to take it. Daily Shift Brief's topics and services do not in any way contribute to OSHA policy or compliance.

Four on four off, rotating twelves, straight days, any rotation. Each crew gets its briefings on the days it actually works. No extra days.
