Privacy policy

Draft - pending attorney review. Not legal advice.

This policy says what SafetyBrief keeps, and what it does not take. It is a draft. The subscription rules are in the terms.

Who we are

The service is called SafetyBrief. The legal entity is not formed yet:

[LLC name - pending formation]

Mailing address:

Email hello@dailyshiftbrief.com with a privacy question.

What we collect

  • Supervisor or account contact name and email
  • Shift End Test results, only if the plant uses that free optional add-on: worker name, crew, date, score, shift, week covered, briefing topics, pass or fail, and the supervisor's attestation
  • Sign-off records: name, crew, and date

The pricing page also asks for your name, company, title, work email, and a second company contact email. Those go only into the Stripe checkout reference or the invoice-request email. This website does not store them.

If you email us, we keep that message so we can reply. We do not collect Social Security numbers or medical info. The Shift End Test results are not on this website yet.

Why

If the plant uses the Shift End Test, the results are a record that a worker answered that week's questions. The test is free and optional. It is not part of the base subscription. SafetyBrief's topics and services do not in any way contribute to OSHA policy or compliance. The account name and email are how we run the subscription and answer mail.

The plant owns and controls its records. SafetyBrief processes them only to provide the service.

Who can see it

The plant's authorized supervisors and admins can see the plant's records. SafetyBrief staff can see them only for support.

How long, and how you take it back

Export and delete are coming soon, when the account is open. The plant will be able to export or delete its records.

[Retention period after cancellation - pending]

We do not sell it

We do not sell personal data. We do not rent it or trade it.

Plant systems

No access to your plant systems. We do not connect to your network, machines, HR system, badge system, or email server. The brief runs in the browser or prints as a PDF. A plant manager can hand IT the security sheet.

Cards

Stripe handles card data. When card checkout is on, you enter the card on a Stripe page, not on this site. We do not store the card number. Stripe's rules are at stripe.com/privacy.

If you pay by invoice, we email it to the account contact. Payment is net-30 by bank transfer (ACH).

Security

This public website does not hold the Shift End Test results. If the plant uses that free add-on and the account is open, only the plant's authorized supervisors and admins, and SafetyBrief staff for support, will be able to open them, and only with a login. We have not published an encryption or MFA setup, because that account is not on this website yet.

We will notify the plant within 10 days of determining a breach of the records we hold for that plant.

Service providers

The host that serves this website. Stripe, for card payments, when checkout is open. The email service that carries mail to and from hello@dailyshiftbrief.com. We have not named a separate host for the Shift End Test results, because that free add-on is not on this site yet.

This website

The public site is static files. It sets no tracking cookies. The pricing page asks for your name, company, title, work email, and a second company contact email. They go only into the Stripe checkout reference or your invoice-request email. The site stores nothing.

Ask us

Email hello@dailyshiftbrief.com to export, correct, or delete what we have.
