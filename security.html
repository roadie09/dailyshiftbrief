What it is

Daily Shift Brief is a daily pre-shift briefing. It covers more than safety. It runs in the browser, or you print it as a PDF. Nothing to install. No VPN. No account on your domain. No firewall change.

What we store

When the account is open, we will store the supervisor or account contact name and email, and the sign-off: name, crew, and date. The Shift End Test is free and optional. If the plant uses it, we will also store worker name, crew, date, and score, plus shift, the week covered, the briefing topics, pass or fail, and the supervisor's attestation. Those Shift End Test results are not on this website yet. Export and delete are coming soon.

The plant owns its records. Export and delete are coming soon. The privacy policy is still a draft.

What we do not store

  • No Social Security numbers
  • No medical info
  • No badge numbers, clock records, or injury reports

Plant systems

No access to your plant systems. We do not connect to your network, your machines, your HR system, your badge system, or your email server. We may email the account contact. We do not log in to your mail server.

If a computer can open a normal website, it can open the brief. If it cannot, print the PDF and carry the paper to the meeting.

Payment

Card checkout, when it is on, is a Stripe page. Stripe handles the card. We do not store the card number. Plants that use accounts payable can request a net-30 invoice and pay by bank transfer (ACH).
