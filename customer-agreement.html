IMPORTANT: DAILY SHIFT BRIEF DOES NOT PREVENT INJURIES OR DEATHS AND IS NOT A SAFETY PROGRAM.

Daily Shift Brief provides short, written safety-awareness briefings based on public OSHA incident reports, together with a sign-off sheet. The Shift End Test is a free optional add-on, at no extra cost, when the customer orders it. It is general information. Its purpose is to help your crews pay attention to hazards. It does not prevent injuries, illnesses, or deaths, and nothing in it promises a safer workplace, fewer incidents, fewer citations, or lower costs.

Daily Shift Brief is not a substitute for your safety and health program, your hazard assessments, your required training, your qualified or competent persons, your safety professionals, or your own judgment. Daily Shift Brief does not inspect, audit, supervise, or control your workplace, equipment, procedures, or workers. It does not take on, and you do not hand over, any duty you owe your employees or anyone else, including your duty to provide a safe workplace and to comply with the Occupational Safety and Health Act, OSHA standards, state plans, and other laws.

THE SHIFT END TEST IS NOT OSHA-REQUIRED TRAINING AND DOES NOT CERTIFY ANYONE.

When the customer orders the optional Shift End Test, at no extra cost, the test and the Shift End Test results show only that a worker answered questions about that week's briefings. They supplement your training. They do not replace it. Does not replace training required by specific OSHA standards. A passing score does not mean a worker is trained, qualified, authorized, or competent to perform any task.
