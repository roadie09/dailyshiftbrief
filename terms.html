The service

Daily Shift Brief is a daily pre-shift briefing for a manufacturing plant. Each brief has what went wrong, the control that would have stopped it, and two questions the crew answers out loud. The supervisor signs a sheet. You can open the brief in a browser or print a PDF. A Spanish version is included.

The brief runs in a browser or prints as a PDF. It does not need access to plant systems.

You subscribe monthly, at the price on the pricing page.

THE SHIFT END TEST IS NOT OSHA-REQUIRED TRAINING AND DOES NOT CERTIFY ANYONE.

When the customer orders the optional Shift End Test, at no extra cost, the test and the Shift End Test results show only that a worker answered questions about that week's briefings. They supplement your training. They do not replace it. They do not meet, and Daily Shift Brief does not say they meet, any training, qualification, certification, evaluation, or recordkeeping requirement under any OSHA standard or other law. Examples of standards with their own requirements include, among others, 29 CFR 1910.147 (lockout/tagout), 29 CFR 1910.178 (powered industrial trucks/forklifts), and 29 CFR 1910.1200 (hazard communication). You must review each briefing

Briefings, questions and images are prepared with the help of automated and AI tools from public sources and may contain errors, omissions, outdated information or content that does not fit your site. Daily Shift Brief tries to make them accurate, but you must have a qualified person at your site review each briefing before use and correct or remove anything that is wrong or does not fit. Do not follow a briefing if it conflicts with your procedures, the equipment manufacturer's instructions, or the law. Incident summaries describe past OSHA reports and do not say anything about any current employer. The heading "The control that would have stopped it" describes that past incident. It is not a promise about your plant.
